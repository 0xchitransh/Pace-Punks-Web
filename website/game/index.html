    How scoring works

    • Distance. One point for every few steps. The run keeps getting faster and the obstacles keep getting closer the longer you last.
    • Chips. Each gold chip you grab adds 25 points.
    • Big jumps. Tap for a hop, hold for a high jump. On a keyboard, press down to drop fast.
    • A long journey. The corridor runs through twelve parts of the station, from the mint concourse and the engine rooms to the labs, the sunset deck, the lounge and the aquarium, each with its own colours. Then it starts over.
    • Watch the drones. Low drones need a jump. High drones only catch you if you jump into them.
    • Fair board. Every ranked run is replayed on the server from your button presses, and the replay decides the score. Edited scores, sped-up games and reused runs are rejected. One spot per runner name.

    Corridor Run is a free mini game. Points here are just for the leaderboard and are not GP.
