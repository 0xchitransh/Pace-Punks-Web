How it works

Your steps are the game controller.

  1. 1

    Move

    Walk for at least 10 minutes. The app verifies each session on your phone, and overlapping data only counts once.

  2. 2

    Score

    Earn up to 35 Movement Score a day. It never resets, and it unlocks your place in each Punk release.

  3. 3

    Earn GP

    Verified minutes earn GP, the in-game currency. Spend it on repairs, levels and upgrades for your Punk.

  4. 4

    Level up

    Climb from level 1 to 30, gain two attribute points per level, then unlock one evolution with a new look.

Your Punk

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Pace Punks free?

Yes. Tracking, clubs, quests and Pace Boxes are free for everyone. New players also get a 28-day GP trial that starts on their first verified walk. A Punk adds a character you level up, but you can play without one.

Do I need a crypto wallet?

No. Early access and the playtest only need an email. Details about owning a Punk come with the first release.

What counts as movement?

During the pilot, walking and supported accessible activity. You need 10 verified minutes in a day to score. Going faster doesn't earn more, so walk at your own pace.

How do you stop cheating?

The app checks every walking minute against your phone's motion sensor and activity type, so shaking the phone, cycling or driving doesn't count. Location is optional and only used for speed, never to store your route. Before rewards go live, the server double-checks walks too, and if one is rejected by mistake you can appeal for seven days.

Is a Punk an investment?

No. Punks are collectible game characters and GP is an in-game currency. We make no promises about resale value, income or token prices. Play because the game is fun.

When can I get a Punk?

After the playtest and pilot. The first release is 600 Punks at $4.65, and you'll need a lifetime Movement Score of 60, about two days of 20+ minute walks.

Which phones will it run on?

iPhone and Android. Tell us which one you use when you sign up so we can invite the right testers.
