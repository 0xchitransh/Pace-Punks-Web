Privacy

Early access privacy notice

Draft notice for the early access list. Have it reviewed for the countries you launch in before going live.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Anything optional you add: X handle, phone type, how you usually move, invite code, and whether you want to join the playtest.
  • The page you signed up from and any campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source or ref).

Why

To email you about Pace Punks early access, the playtest, app downloads and releases, and to pick playtesters across iPhone and Android.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. This website doesn't collect any movement or location data. There are no ads or tracking tools on the site or in the app.

In the app

The app asks for permission before it reads anything. During a walk it uses steps, the motion sensor and the phone's activity type (walking, cycling, in a vehicle) to score the walk and filter out shaking, cycling and driving. Location is optional, only used while you walk, and only to check speed and distance. You can turn it off in Profile, and indoor walks still count.

In preview mode, all of this stays on your phone. When you sign in to earn rewards, we store:

  • Your email address, used to sign in with a 6-digit code.
  • For each walk, per-minute step counts and motion details: intensity, rhythm, activity type, GPS speed and distance. Never your location coordinates or route.
  • A hashed ID of your phone, the app's device key and the phone model, so each phone has one account.
  • The IP address you signed up from, to spot fake account farms.

We use this only to verify walks, prevent cheating and run the game. We don't sell it or use it for ads.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. That removes everything above except the hashed phone ID, which we keep so a phone can't be reset to create new accounts.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To have your signup deleted, email us at the address listed in our social profiles and we'll remove it.
